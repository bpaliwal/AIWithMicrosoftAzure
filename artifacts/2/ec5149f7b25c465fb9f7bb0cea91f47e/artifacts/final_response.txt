According to Air India’s waiver policy, a **domestic flight reschedule qualifies for waiver/reissue when it is rescheduled for more than 1 hour**. This is stated in **Source 1: ai-schedule-change-policy-updated.pdf**.

For **full refund** on a cancelled flight, the policy notes that the **cancellation charge is waived** and the agent can process the refund, but the document does **not clearly state a separate “full refund” time threshold for domestic reschedules**.
