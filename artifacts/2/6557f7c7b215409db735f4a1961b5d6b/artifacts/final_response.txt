Based on the available information, a travel voucher issued in these circumstances must be:

- **Valid for at least five years from the date of issuance**
- **Transferable**

This is stated in **Source 5** of the Knowledge Base, which says airlines must issue travel credits or vouchers with those characteristics when a passenger cannot travel due to certain serious communicable disease-related reasons.

The Knowledge Base does **not** provide any additional mandatory voucher characteristics beyond those two.
