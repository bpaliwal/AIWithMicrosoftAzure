Yes—**a ticket agent may retain a service fee in some cases**, even if a flight is cancelled and the passenger is entitled to a refund, **so long as the fee is for more than just processing payment for the flight the consumer found**. The fee must be:

- **on a per-passenger basis**, and
- **clearly and prominently disclosed** to consumers at the time they purchase the airfare.

The Knowledge Base also says that **undisclosed fees are considered deceptive** by the Department’s Office of Aviation Consumer Protection.  
**Source:** U.S. Department of Transportation - *Refunds and Other Consumer Protections.pdf* (Sources 3, 4, and 5)
