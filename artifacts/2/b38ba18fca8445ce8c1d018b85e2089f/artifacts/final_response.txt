For an international flight segment between the United States and a foreign point that is **over 12 hours** in duration, DOT rules say a baggage fee refund is due if the airline does **not** deliver the checked bag within **30 hours** of the flight arriving at the gate, and the passenger has filed a Mishandled Baggage Report.

So for a **14-hour international flight**, the mandatory refund threshold is **30 hours**.
