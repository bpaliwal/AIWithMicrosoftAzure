Yes, but only in limited cases.

According to the Air India Conditions of Carriage, **firearms and ammunition for hunting and sporting purposes may be accepted as Checked Baggage**, in accordance with the carrier’s regulations. The firearms must be **unloaded, with the safety catch on, and suitably packed**. Ammunition carriage is also subject to **ICAO and IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations**.  
**Source:** air-india-coc.pdf, section 9.1.2

However, **firearms and ammunition other than for hunting and sporting purposes are prohibited from carriage as baggage**.  
**Source:** air-india-coc.pdf, section 9.1.2
