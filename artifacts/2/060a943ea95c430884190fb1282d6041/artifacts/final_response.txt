If your flight is cancelled, the available information says the airline may handle it in a few ways:

- **Air India conditions of carriage:** If the airline cancels your flight, it may:
  - carry you on another scheduled service with available space,
  - reroute you to your destination on its own or another carrier’s services, or by surface transportation,
  - or extend the validity of your ticket until the airline’s first available flight in the same class of service.  
  **Source:** Air India conditions of carriage (Sources 3 and 4)

- **If the cancellation is due to bankruptcy:** Bankruptcy can affect refund rights. The airline or ticket agent may be temporarily prohibited from providing refunds or vouchers. If you paid by credit card and the airline refuses to refund you, you may be entitled to a credit from your credit card company under the Fair Credit Billing Act. If you purchased travel insurance, contact your insurer to see whether you are eligible for compensation.  
  **Source:** U.S. Department of Transportation (Sources 1 and 2)
