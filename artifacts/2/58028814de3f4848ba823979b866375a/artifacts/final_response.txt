According to the Air India Conditions of Carriage, **excess baggage** is baggage that is **in excess of the free baggage allowance**.

- A passenger **must pay charges** for carrying excess baggage.
- The **rate and manner of payment** are prescribed in the carrier’s regulations.  
  **Source:** *air-india-coc.pdf, Article 9.6 “Excess Baggage”*

The available information also says that:
- **Free baggage allowance** is carried free of charge, subject to the carrier’s regulations and limitations.  
  **Source:** *air-india-coc.pdf, Article 9.5*
- If the carrier offers an **excess value declaration** facility, a passenger may declare a higher value for checked baggage and pay any applicable charges.  
  **Source:** *air-india-coc.pdf, Article 9.7.1*
