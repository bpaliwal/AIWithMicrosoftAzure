For an international flight segment between the United States and a foreign point that is **over 12 hours** in duration, DOT rules say a **mandatory baggage fee refund** is due if the airline does not deliver the checked bag within **30 hours** of the flight arriving at the gate, and the passenger has filed a Mishandled Baggage Report.

Relevant source: **U.S. Department of Transportation - Refunds and Other Consumer Protections.pdf** (Source 2).
