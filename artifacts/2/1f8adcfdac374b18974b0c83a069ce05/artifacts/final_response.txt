According to the Air India conditions of carriage, the following items should **not be included in checked baggage**:

- **Fragile or perishable items**
- **Money**
- **Jewelry**
- **Precious metals**
- **Silverware**
- **Negotiable papers**
- **Securities**
- **Other valuables**
- **Business documents**
- **Passports and other identification documents**
- **Samples**

Also, items that are **prohibited from carriage as baggage** include:
- Items that are **not considered baggage**
- Items that may **endanger the aircraft, persons, or property** (including dangerous goods)
- Items **prohibited by applicable laws or regulations**
- **Live animals** (except as provided in the conditions)
- **Firearms and ammunition**, except hunting/sporting firearms and ammunition accepted under carrier regulations

Relevant sources: **air-india-coc.pdf** (Sources 1, 2, and 3).
