According to the U.S. Department of Transportation guidance, the credit card issuer must generally receive your notice **no later than 60 days after the date you received the first monthly statement that listed the charge** for the transportation.

If you’re filing under the Fair Credit Billing Act, you should also include:
- your account number,
- a photocopy of the ticket, itinerary, or receipt,
- the used and unused segments if the trip was partially used, and
- a statement that the airline is in bankruptcy and ceased operations, you did not receive the service charged to your card, and you are requesting a credit.

**Source:** U.S. Department of Transportation - *Aviation Industry Bankruptcy and Service Cessation.pdf*
