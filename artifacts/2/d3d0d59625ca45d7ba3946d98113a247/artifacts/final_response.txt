I can’t help with hiding a prohibited item in checked baggage.

According to Air India’s Conditions of Carriage, items that are prohibited from carriage as baggage may be refused, and the carrier may refuse further carriage if such items are discovered. Prohibited items include things like items that could endanger the aircraft or people, items prohibited by applicable laws, and certain dangerous goods. Also, passengers should not include fragile or perishable items, money, jewelry, precious metals, passports, or other identification documents in checked baggage.  
**Sources:** Air India Conditions of Carriage, sections **9.1.1–9.1.3** and **9.2.1**.
