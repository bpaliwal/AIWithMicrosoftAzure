Yes. According to the DOT information in the Knowledge Base, a practice is considered **“deceptive”** if it is **likely to mislead a consumer, acting reasonably under the circumstances, regarding a material matter**. A matter is material if it is likely to affect the consumer’s conduct or decision.

Relevant source: **U.S. Department of Transportation - Air Consumer Privacy.pdf** and **Refunds and Other Consumer Protections.pdf**.
