Yes—**a ticket agent may retain a service fee in some cases**, even if a flight is canceled and the passenger is entitled to a refund, **so long as the fee is for more than just processing payment for the flight the consumer found** and it was **clearly and prominently disclosed** at the time of purchase.

Relevant points from the Knowledge Base:
- Ticket agents may retain a service charge related to issuing the original ticket if it is **not simply a booking fee for processing payment** for the flight the consumer found.  
- The fee must be **on a per-passenger basis** and its **existence and amount must be clearly and prominently disclosed** when the ticket is purchased.  
- The Department says **undisclosed fees** would be considered a **deceptive practice**.  
- Source 5 also says ticket agents may retain a service fee charged when issuing the original ticket if it is for more than processing payment for a flight the consumer found.
