What the Knowledge Base does provide from Air India’s conditions of carriage is:

- **Items not allowed as baggage** include:
  - items that are not considered baggage,
  - items that may endanger the aircraft, people, or property,
  - items prohibited by applicable laws,
  - items unsuitable because of weight, size, or character,
  - live animals, except as otherwise provided,
  - firearms and ammunition except for hunting/sporting purposes under specific conditions,
  - fragile or perishable items, money, jewelry, precious metals, silverware, negotiable papers, securities, valuables, business documents, passports, and other identification documents.  
  **Source:** air-india-coc.pdf, Article 9.1

- **Checked baggage** is tagged by the carrier, and if baggage lacks identification, the passenger must attach it before acceptance. Checked baggage is normally carried on the same aircraft, if practicable.  
  **Source:** air-india-coc.pdf, Article 9.4

- **Free baggage allowance** is available “as specified” and subject to the carrier’s regulations, but the actual allowance amount is not given in the provided text.  
  **Source:** air-india-coc.pdf, Article 9.5

- **Excess baggage** charges apply if baggage exceeds the free allowance, at rates set in the carrier’s regulations.  
  **Source:** air-india-coc.pdf, Article 9.6
