If your baggage is delayed on an international itinerary, the available information says a **“significantly delayed checked bag”** is one that is not delivered to you or picked up by someone authorized on your behalf within:

- **15 hours** of the last flight segment’s arrival for **international itineraries with a non-stop flight segment between the United States and a foreign point that is 12 hours or less in duration**
- **30 hours** of the last flight segment’s arrival for **international itineraries with a non-stop flight segment between the United States and a foreign point that is more than 12 hours in duration**

The **15-hour and 30-hour standards apply to domestic segments of international itineraries** as well.  
(Source: U.S. Department of Transportation - *Refunds and Other Consumer Protections.pdf*)

The Knowledge Base also says carriers are expected to make every reasonable effort to return mishandled baggage within the applicable timeframes, and they may compensate consumers for incidental expenses related to delayed bags, subject to liability limits. However, it does not provide more detail on exactly what you should do next.
