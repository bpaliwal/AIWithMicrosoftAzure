According to the Knowledge Base, DOT enforces COPPA with respect to airlines, and DOT shares jurisdiction with the FTC over ticket agents regarding COPPA. The DOT’s Air Consumer Privacy information also says that COPPA prohibits websites from obtaining personal information from children under 13 without parental consent, and that the FTC has issued the implementing regulations.

Relevant source: U.S. Department of Transportation - Air Consumer Privacy.pdf (Sources 1 and 3).
