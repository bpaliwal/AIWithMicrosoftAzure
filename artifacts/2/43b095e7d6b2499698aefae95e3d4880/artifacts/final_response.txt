For international flights, the available information says:

- **Free baggage allowance:** Passengers may carry baggage **free of charge** as specified, **subject to the conditions and limitations in the carrier’s regulations**.  
  - **Source:** *air-india-coc.pdf* (Article 9.5)

- **Excess baggage:** If your baggage exceeds the free allowance, you must **pay excess baggage charges** at the rate and in the manner prescribed in the carrier’s regulations.  
  - **Source:** *air-india-coc.pdf* (Article 9.6)

- **Checked baggage handling:** Checked baggage is normally carried on the **same aircraft as the passenger**, unless that is impracticable, in which case it may be carried on the **next available flight**.  
  - **Source:** *air-india-coc.pdf* (Article 9.4.3)

The knowledge base does **not** provide the exact international baggage allowance amount or piece/weight limits. It only states that the allowance depends on the carrier’s regulations.
