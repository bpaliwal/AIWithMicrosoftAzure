According to the DOT’s final rule, a **significant change** for an **international flight’s arrival time** is when the passenger is scheduled to **arrive at the destination airport 6 hours or more later than the original scheduled arrival time**.  

This is stated in the DOT’s *Refunds and Other Consumer Protections* rule, which defines significant change of flight itinerary for international itineraries as a **6-hour or more** arrival delay.
