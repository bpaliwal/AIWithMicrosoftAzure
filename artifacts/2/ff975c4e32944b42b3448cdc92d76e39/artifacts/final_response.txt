According to the U.S. Department of Transportation guidance, if you paid by credit card and are seeking a refund under the Fair Credit Billing Act, the credit card issuer should generally receive your dispute notice **no later than 60 days after the date you received the first monthly statement that listed the charge**.

You should also send it to the address on your monthly statement for disputing charges and include:
- your account number,
- a photocopy of the ticket, itinerary, or receipt,
- the used and unused segments if the trip was partially used, and
- a statement that the airline is in bankruptcy and ceased operations, you did not receive the service charged, and you are requesting a credit under the Fair Credit Billing Act.

Source: **U.S. Department of Transportation - Aviation Industry Bankruptcy and Service Cessation.pdf**
